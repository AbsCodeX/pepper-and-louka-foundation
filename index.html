About Pepper & Louka

A real story of care, community, and standing up for what's right.

The Rescue

In May 2026, Louka Berman found a baby raccoon shot with a crossbow at a Montréal construction site. He nursed Pepper back to health with the intention of releasing her.

The Bond

Pepper kept returning. Their connection went viral. Locals called Louka Montréal’s “raccoon guy.”

The Fight

Wildlife authorities sought to seize her. Louka is fighting charges and working to build a sanctuary while the community supports them through $PEPPER.
