About Pepper & Louka

In May 2026, Montréal resident Louka Berman found a baby raccoon shot with a crossbow at a construction site. The mother had already died. Louka nursed Pepper back to health, intending to return her to the wild. Every time he tried to release her, Pepper stayed and waited for him.

Videos of their bond went viral. Then, in early October 2026, Québec wildlife authorities served a search warrant. Pepper went into hiding. Louka faces charges and fears what could happen if she is seized. The community responded. A Solana token called $PEPPER was created, with 100% of creator fees going to Louka to help build a sanctuary and protect Pepper.
